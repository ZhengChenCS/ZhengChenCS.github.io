I am currently a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Department of Computer Science and Technology of Tsinghua University, working with Professor [Jidong Zhai](https://pacman.cs.tsinghua.edu.cn/~zjd/#about). I received my Ph.D. degree in 2025 and B.S. degree in 2020 from the School of Information, Renmin University of China.

My research interests include high performance computing, big data management systems, graph analytics, graph compression, databases, and machine learning systems.

Before joining Tsinghua University, I was a Ph.D. student advised by Professor [Feng Zhang](https://fengzhangcs.github.io/) and Professor Xiaoyong Du. I was also a visiting student at the School of Computing, National University of Singapore, advised by Professor [Bingsheng He](https://www.comp.nus.edu.sg/~hebs/).
