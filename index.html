Postdoctoral Researcher

Zheng Chen 陈政

Department of Computer Science and Technology, Tsinghua University

I am currently a Postdoctoral Researcher at Tsinghua University, working with Professor Jidong Zhai. I received my Ph.D. degree in 2025 and B.S. degree in 2020 from the School of Information, Renmin University of China.

My research interests include high performance computing, big data management systems, graph analytics, graph compression, databases, and machine learning systems.
